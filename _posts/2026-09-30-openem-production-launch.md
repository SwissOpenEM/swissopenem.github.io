**OpenEM is live in production!** This milestone brings our shared infrastructure
for electron microscopy data into operational use, supporting FAIR data management
across the OpenEM community.

## A refreshed website and revised documentation

Alongside the production launch, we have updated the
[OpenEM website](https://www.openem.ch/) and revised the
[documentation](https://data-catalog-services.pages.psi.ch/openem/openem-start/).
Visit the website to explore OpenEM and find your way to the services, and use the
documentation for guidance on getting started and working with your data.

Ready to explore? Browse publicly available electron microscopy datasets through
[discovery.psi.ch](https://discovery.psi.ch/) to see data shared by the community.

## Coming in the next release

The next release is already planned and will include the **Depositor release**
and **Ingestor improvements**. These updates will follow in a separate release;
we will share further details as it approaches.
