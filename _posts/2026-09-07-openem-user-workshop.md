Join us for the **OpenEM User Workshop 2026** on 7 September 2026, from 15:00 to 18:00 CEST.

The workshop is open to users of ETH Zurich (ScopeM), EPFL/UNIL (DCI-L and LBEM), the
University of Basel (BioEM), the University of Bern (DCI-B), the University of Geneva
(DCI-G), PSI (EMF), Empa (EM Center), and the wider electron microscopy community.

This hybrid event will introduce how to archive, share, and publish electron microscopy
datasets according to FAIR data management principles. The programme includes an
introduction to OpenEM, a live demonstration, breakout groups, and an open discussion.

## Practical information

- **Date:** 7 September 2026, 15:00-18:00 CEST
- **Location:** Seminarraum 115, University of Bern Hauptgebäude
- **Format:** In person or online via Microsoft Teams
