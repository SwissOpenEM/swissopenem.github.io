## Support

For help using OpenEM, start with your **facility operator**, who can set up your
account, add you to your lab's group, and help with ingesting data.

For questions, bug reports and feature requests, write to the
[OpenEM support mailing list](https://psilists.ethz.ch/sympa/info/openem-help)
(`openem-help@lists.psi.ch`).

You can also reach the team and other users on
[Slack](https://join.slack.com/t/openem/shared_invite/zt-2zol5byg0-hQPsS3xb_CPsNk7QsbY8tw)
or [GitHub](https://github.com/SwissOpenEM).

## News and announcements

Subscribe to the
[OpenEM members mailing list](https://psilists.ethz.ch/sympa/info/openem-members)
to receive newsletters and announcements about OpenEM. Past newsletters are on the
[News]({{ '/news/' | relative_url }}) page.

## Documentation

Guides for users, facility operators and developers are in the
[OpenEM documentation](https://data-catalog-services.pages.psi.ch/openem/openem-start/).
